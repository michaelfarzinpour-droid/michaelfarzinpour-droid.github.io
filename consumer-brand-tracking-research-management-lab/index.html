Competitive diagnostics

Interpretation guide

Awareness captures recognition, consideration option-set inclusion, preference first choice, purchase intent near-term likelihood, and advocacy propensity to recommend. These are not a mathematically ordered conversion funnel because each is asked of all eligible respondents.

Wave-over-wave changes describe simulated cross-sectional snapshots, not the behavior of the same people over time.

QUESTIONNAIRE · SAMPLING · WEIGHTING

Questionnaire builder

Sampling & quota plan

U.S. adults 18–64 who are involved in smartphone purchase decisions; illustrative online-panel recruitment. Four independent quarterly cross-sections, 600 completes per wave; total N=2,400 before any sensitivity exclusions.

Quota margins target balance on age and gender; additional monitoring on region. Synthetic dataset is not probability-sampled and does not support population inference.

Weighting specification

Demonstration poststratification via gender × age group. Each cell’s raw counts are adjusted toward fixed, illustrative targets; individual adjustment factors are capped at 0.5–2.0, then normalized to each wave’s unweighted sample size.

Targets are fictional planning assumptions, not Census estimates. Production requires verified benchmarks, design-effect review, and sensitivity analysis.

Fieldwork monitoring protocol

01 Soft launch — validate routing, mobile UX, time distributions, and early quota mix at 10%.
02 Daily controls — quotas, source mix, completes vs target, incidence, device, and adverse quality signals.
03 Escalation — hold suspicious supplier batches, investigate panel IDs, document decisions, and request replacements.
04 Closeout — lock data dictionary, exclusion log, weights, base-size tables and report QA signoff.
SPSS-STYLE CROSSTABS · SIGNIFICANCE

Illustrative pairwise unpooled two-proportion z tests versus the first displayed subgroup, unadjusted p values, nominal 95% Wald intervals. Independent observations assumed. Weighting and design effects are not incorporated into the test variance; use complex-sample methods for real inference. Multiple comparisons and small cells increase false-positive risk.

Interpretation discipline

Distinguish a point estimate from a reliable difference. Evaluate base sizes, magnitude, uncertainty, repeatability across waves, weighting sensitivity, and practical implications before calling a movement meaningful.

Analysis workflow

Freeze analysis plan → validate denominators → tabulate by segment and brand → run comparison tests → review small cells and multiplicity → document sensitivity → develop recommendations.

My recommended approach

I would run the engagement as a decision-oriented research program, with a documented study charter, explicit quality gates, visible ownership, and executive storytelling that clearly separates evidence from inference.

Sample client communication

We will share an impact assessment, updated schedule and written disposition before dataset lock.”
