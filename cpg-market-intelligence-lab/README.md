# CPG Market Intelligence & eCommerce Analytics Lab

**Independent portfolio simulation — all brands, retailers, sales, survey responses, and scenarios are synthetic. Not affiliated with The Wonderful Company.**

[Open the interactive simulation](./index.html)

## Scope
- 240 synthetic brand × retailer × month sales records for 2026
- 16 synthetic quarterly brand tracker records
- Interactive filtering by brand, month and channel
- Retail sales, units, category share, online revenue mix, attributed ROAS and eCommerce conversion
- Two guided sales-decline decision exercises with diagnostic feedback
- Exportable synthetic executive brief and filtered data

## How to use
Open `index.html` in a browser. The dashboard runs locally without dependencies or an API. Use the filters, inspect the retail/eCommerce views, try the decision exercise, and download the executive summary.

Data: [sales CSV](./synthetic_cpg_monthly.csv) · [consumer tracker CSV](./synthetic_consumer_tracker.csv). Build a native Power BI report using the accompanying guide.

## Interpretation and limitations
All metrics are derived from generated records. Attributed sales divided by spend is a descriptive ROAS ratio **not incremental advertising lift**. Category share uses a synthetic category denominator. Online conversion uses orders divided by visits. The distribution scenario is fictional and does not establish a real-world causal effect. There is no licensed Circana/Nielsen feed, real brand performance, fielded consumer survey, or native Power BI dashboard in this repository.
