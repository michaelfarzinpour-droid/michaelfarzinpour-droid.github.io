What the measures mean

ROAS: attributed sales ÷ ad spend. An attribution measure, not causal evidence.

Conversion: online orders ÷ site visits, for online observations only.

Category share: simulated brand sales ÷ simulated category sales.

Do not compare the simulated values to real syndicated data or actual company financials.
