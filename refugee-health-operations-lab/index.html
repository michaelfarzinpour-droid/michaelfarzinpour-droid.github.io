Illustrative management briefing

Prepared by Michael Farzinpour · Independent simulated exercise · Not an official agency document

Executive assessment

A fictional one-month operations review contains 250 eligibility records, 18 records flagged for supporting-document verification, 7 claims flagged for potential duplication, and 4 invoices requiring reconciliation. These are separate screening queues, not confirmed errors. The immediate priority is to establish evidence sufficiency, prevent unsupported financial adjustments, and refer policy ambiguities to authorized owners.

Priority actions

  1. Eligibility: obtain source documentation and reconcile qualifying dates; do not infer benefit status from missing fields alone.
  2. Claims: request adjustment histories and payment status for potential duplicates before pursuing reimbursement changes.
  3. Fiscal: trace unsupported invoice line items to accepted contract deliverables and route findings to designated approvers.
  4. Policy: reconcile the October 1, 2026 ORR notice with state implementation materials; secure ORH/DHCS interpretation and document the approved procedure version.

Decision log

No case decisions evaluated yet. Complete the decision modules to populate the log.

Proposed controls and follow-through

For each exception, maintain a review owner, evidence request, decision authority, due date, disposition, and recheck date. Escalate unresolved source conflicts; preserve an audit trail; publish aggregate management indicators without sensitive beneficiary information.

Limitations

All cases, amounts, queue counts, and contract milestones are fictional. There is no live policy or claims connection. The exercise evaluates analytical process, not authorization to determine eligibility, reimbursement or contract payment.

Methods & reference materials

The demonstration uses a fixed, purpose-built set of fictional scenarios. The 250-person count and exception counts are illustrative and are not statistical estimates, outcome measurements, or a reproduction of a governmental data system. Choices are evaluated against a transparent operational rubric: seek evidence, observe decision authority, document controls, and avoid premature findings.

What was demonstrated

Issue triage, risk-based prioritization, claims exception review, invoice reconciliation, policy source comparison, corrective-action documentation, management communication, and reproducible mock review extracts. No sensitive data are processed or stored; browser decisions remain local to the page.

Public reference sources

  • CDPH Office of Refugee Health — Program Overview (state program responsibilities; page displayed eight months at the time of preparation).
  • DHCS — Immigration Status and Changes to Medi-Cal Eligibility (RMA and federally funded Medi-Cal context).
