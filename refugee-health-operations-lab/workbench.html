Data dictionary and reproducibility

Beneficiary keys (BEN-###), claim keys (CLM-###), and invoice keys (INV-###) are fictitious. Generated categories, amounts, and dates are illustrative. flag means only that a predefined practice scenario needs review. difference equals submitted minus supported amount. Claims are flagged when they share a simulated beneficiary and service key with an earlier claim; this is only an investigation trigger. All counts and totals are computed in this browser from the displayed arrays.

Policy-source reconciliation

Where published guidance differs, the appropriate analytical action is to log the source, date, applicability, policy owner and resolution evidence—not to decide eligibility from the demonstration. Verify approved current guidance with CDPH, DHCS, and the federal Office of Refugee Resettlement.
