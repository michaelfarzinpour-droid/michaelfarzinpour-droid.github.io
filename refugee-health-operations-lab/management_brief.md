# Refugee Health Program Operations Lab — Illustrative Management Brief

**Michael Farzinpour, PhD | October 2026**

*Independent portfolio simulation using fictional cases and amounts. Not affiliated with CDPH, DHCS, or ORR.*

## Executive assessment

A one-month fictional operations review includes **250 beneficiary records**, **18 documentation flags**, **7 potential duplicate-claim flags**, and **4 invoices in reconciliation review**. These are separate review queues, not confirmed errors or counts of distinct affected individuals.

## Recommended actions

- **Eligibility:** Obtain verified qualifying-date records and perform partner checks before reaching eligibility conclusions.
- **Claims:** Review payment status, adjustments, and reversal history prior to any reimbursement correction.
- **Fiscal:** Reconcile the fictional **$3,600** unsupported invoice amount to accepted contract deliverables; route through authorized approvals.
- **Compliance:** Document the October 1, 2026 federal notice describing a 12-month RMA period and a CDPH webpage describing eight months; confirm the current approved state implementation with policy owners.

## Corrective-action control register

| Issue | Proposed owner | Closeout evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility documentation | Eligibility partner | Verified source + case log |
| Claims exception | Claims partner | Disposition + payment history |
| Invoice variance | Fiscal reviewer | Reconciliation + approval trail |
| Source discrepancy | ORH/DHCS policy owners | Approved guidance + version log |

## Evidence and limitations

All records, counts, amounts, and scenarios are invented for skills demonstration. The lab does not implement actual eligibility criteria, claim payment rules, or contract terms. Policy changes and applicability require authoritative program interpretation.
