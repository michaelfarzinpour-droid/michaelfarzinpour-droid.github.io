Your assignment

You are the communications lead for a fictional regional education research consortium releasing an observational study of student persistence. A six-week dissemination campaign must serve college administrators, practitioners, policymakers, and students. Every decision must balance evidence integrity, accessibility, stakeholder trust, and practical delivery.

Simulation boundaries: All partners, data, budgets, scenarios, dashboard figures, and consequences are invented for portfolio demonstration. No real campaign occurred. Simulated scores reflect transparent illustrative rules, not validated evaluation instruments.

Evidence summary

Fictional observational data suggest that learners reporting clearer advising pathways also report higher persistence. This is an association, not evidence that advising caused persistence.

Campaign conditions

Six weeks, a small shared-services team, overlapping client requests, a limited production budget, and multiple publication and accessibility review gates.
