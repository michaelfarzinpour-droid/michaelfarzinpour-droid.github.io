# Investment prioritization framework
Illustrative fictional decision model. Agree weights with regional and enterprise stakeholders before scoring.
| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Alumni-reported need | 30% | Survey plus interviews, coverage and missingness |
| Outcome/evaluation readiness | 25% | Defined endpoint, baseline, feasible comparison |
| Equity and access | 20% | Accessibility, opt-in, participation barriers |
| Feasibility | 15% | Staffing, technology and budget |
| Evidence/data quality | 10% | Reliable denominators, consent and traceability |
Score each option 1–5 against documented criteria; weighted score = sum(weight × score). Review sensitivity to weights and disagreement. Never score individual alumni. Example options: bounded coaching pilot, data quality improvement, mentor recruitment.
Capacity assumptions: $600 per coaching seat, $150 per data remediation case. These are fictional unit costs, not market estimates.
Decision gates: ≥85% follow-up, explicit consent, valid endpoint, subgroup review, impact uncertainty and cost. Defer scaling if evidence is incomplete; assign an accountable owner and 90-day review.
