# Three-year alumni insights and measurement strategy
Fictional leadership proposal; targets are illustrative planning gates, not promises.
| Horizon | Questions | Deliverables | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | Who can be reached, what outcomes are measurable? | CRM key, consent register, annual survey, baseline dashboard, regional data stewards | Quarterly leadership learning review |
| Year 2 | Which career supports serve different alumni needs? | Fixed-cohort transitions, mixed-methods listening, bounded randomized pilot, outcome coverage dashboard | Quarterly regional meetings; six-month pilot review |
| Year 3 | What investments merit scale or redesign? | Replication, subgroup coverage, cost-per-seat, outcome robustness and sustainable operating model | Annual investment review |
## Metric specifications
- CRM coverage: unique canonical IDs / expected alumni population; reconcile duplicates.
- Survey response: unique complete responses / eligible alumni at snapshot date.
- Employment: observed employed / observed career outcome records; separately report follow-up.
- Leadership activity: observed active / observed leadership data; no proxy for aspiration.
- Engagement: observed engaged / known engagement records; no missing-as-zero.
- Invitation eligibility: explicit consent, verified contact, stated pathway interest, experience criteria. Staff reconfirm, no automatic selection.
- Pilot impact: assigned groups at prespecified endpoint, attrition, balance and missing outcome bounds.
## Governance and learning
Quarterly dictionary release; changes approved by evaluation, technology and regional owners. Track denominators, definitions, period and change notes. Report small-cell suppression and bias. Escalate if outcome coverage <85% or permission unclear. Document decisions and owners; revise after feedback.
