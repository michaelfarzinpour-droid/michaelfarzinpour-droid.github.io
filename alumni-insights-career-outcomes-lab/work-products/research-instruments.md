# Alumni experience and career aspirations instruments
Independent fictional research plan. No real participant data.
## Survey (annual; consent screen before questions)
Purpose: improve career services and understand outcomes. Participation is voluntary. Allow "prefer not to answer" for every question; no service eligibility effect. State retention, contact and correction terms before launch. Separate consent for future invitations.
1. Verify reporting year and preferred region (six regions / other / prefer not).
2. Current employment status (full-time / part-time / self-employed / seeking / not seeking / prefer not).
3. Sector (education / public service / nonprofit / private / other).
4. Has your role changed since the prior year? (yes / no / unsure); optional description of role change.
5. Current leadership activity (yes/no; optional type).
6. Which career aspiration is most important now? (transition / leadership / mentoring / professional development / other).
7. Main barrier (time / network access / geography / skills training / no barrier / other).
8. Career Center participation in past 12 months (yes/no/unsure).
9. Satisfaction with services (1–5; not applicable).
10. What would improve your ability to meet your career goals? (open text).
11. Which invitations would you welcome? (mentor / board / advocacy / none; multiple).
12. Permission to contact (explicit yes/no; separate from survey participation).
Pretest with diverse alumni, cognitive interviews, accessible mobile layout and skip logic. Track invitations, partial completions, contactability and response denominator. Do not silently recode missing values.
## Interview guide (30 minutes)
Intro: voluntary, allow recording only with permission; no identifying quotes without consent. Ask what changed since leaving the program; how career goals evolved; supports used and barriers encountered; what would make mentoring or leadership accessible; examples of helpful and unhelpful interactions; preferred engagement and feedback channels. Probe without leading; summarize for participant confirmation.
## Codebook
| Primary code | Include | Exclude | Illustrative scripted example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | Scheduling, workload | Location only | Evening commitments prevent participation |
| Network access | Lack of relevant contacts | Travel distance | Few contacts outside my sector |
| Geography | Physical access or distance | Scheduling alone | Remote options needed |
| Skills training | Learning or career translation | Network only | Need help describing experience |
| No barrier | Explicit absence of barrier | Missing response | Flexible options already work |
Double-code 20% independently in a real study; calculate reliability before reconciliation; maintain a decision log and report discordant/negative cases. No reliability estimate from scripted examples.
