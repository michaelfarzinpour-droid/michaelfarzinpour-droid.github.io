## Cadence
Weeks 1–2: regional listening sessions; collect decisions, current workflows and pain points.
Weeks 3–4: shared data dictionary working session; agree core measures and optional local modules.
Monthly: issue register, owners, deadlines, coverage, adoption and open decisions.
Quarterly: learning review with leadership; agree change requests and decision record.
## Escalation
Quarantine duplicate/unmatched keys; reconcile canonical source before release. Consent uncertainty blocks outreach. Restrict record-level access and log access changes. Report only aggregates with adequate cell sizes. Confirm deletion/retention rules and correction pathway before collecting.
## Meeting agenda
Decision needed; current evidence and uncertainty; local context; feasible options; tradeoffs; decision, owner, due date; revisit date.
