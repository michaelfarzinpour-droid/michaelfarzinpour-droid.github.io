Recommended evaluation protocol

  1. Pre-register a primary outcome: 12-week assessment completion, with time-to-completion as secondary.
  2. Assign eligible learners to intervention versus usual experience, if randomized allocation is feasible and approved; otherwise use a carefully matched comparison and clearly state residual confounding.
  3. Check fidelity and equity with reach, usage, support response times, and subgroup results; avoid using a single aggregate score as proof of equitable effects.
  4. Combine methods through brief survey items, opt-in interviews, and administrative indicators to learn why outcomes may shift.
  5. Apply safeguards: permission and privacy review, minimum-necessary data, access controls, retention limits, and small-cell suppression.
  6. Decide: scale, revise, or discontinue based on predefined thresholds, uncertainty, implementation cost, and potential unintended consequences.

Methods, limitations & source context

Study design (illustrative)

  • Setting: fictional online, competency-oriented certificate program for adult learners.
  • Sample: 600 deterministic synthetic learner records generated locally in the browser, including student context, simulated barriers, and a 12-week assessment-completion indicator.
  • Quantitative: descriptive rates and subgroup comparisons; no statistical causal inference is claimed.
  • Qualitative: four fabricated example comments tagged with a simple descriptive codebook; not actual fieldwork.
  • Evaluation: adjustable two-arm scenario with normal-approximation uncertainty, intended for explanation rather than sample-size certification.
  • Data policy: no personal data is collected, uploaded or transmitted; CSV export is synthetic only.
