Northstar Brand Tracker Questionnaire

Synthetic portfolio instrument. Fictional brands and response options; estimated median length 12 minutes core + 3 minutes rotating module.

1. Screening and category status

  1. Age and market eligibility
  2. Category purchase involvement
  3. Current ownership, recent purchase, and switching openness
  4. Security and attention confirmation

2. Brand funnel

  1. Unaided awareness: “Which brands come to mind?”
  2. Aided awareness: randomized brand list
  3. Consideration: multi-select among aware brands
  4. Preference: single preferred brand
  5. Purchase intent: five-point likelihood scale
  6. Advocacy: likelihood to recommend

3. Brand imagery and experience

  1. Reliable, innovative, easy to use, worth the price, understands me
  2. Setup, support, product design, and trust diagnostics
  3. Open end: “What is the main reason for your current opinion?”

4. Wave 4 rotating module

  1. Recent activation or setup experience
  2. Barrier checklist and primary barrier
  3. Two randomized differentiation messages
  4. Clarity, relevance, credibility, and motivation ratings

QA specification

Test all terminations, mobile layouts, randomization, piping, exclusive options, missing-value codes, and export labels. Soft launch at n=60 before full release.
