My recommended approach

Manage the decision, not only the study.

I would make the business decision, research choices, quality controls, and communication cadence explicit from the beginning.

01

Kickoff around the decision

Define the business decision, stakeholders, success criteria, non-negotiable tracker measures, and open questions. Convert them into a decision-to-questionnaire crosswalk.

02

Lock the operating plan

Assign owners, milestones, dependencies, quality thresholds, escalation paths, vendor expectations, budget checkpoints, and client update cadence before fielding.

03

Protect trend integrity

Hold the core instrument, sample frame, quota logic, and weighting approach stable. Document every change and test whether it could explain movement.

04

Interrogate the signal

Move from headline to segment, competitor, funnel stage, and open text. Test significance and sensitivity, then separate observed evidence from interpretation.

05

Build the story with the client

Use working sessions to pressure-test implications. Tailor analyst, client, and executive views while keeping one auditable evidence base.

06

Close with action and learning

Document decisions, owners, dates, and follow-up measures. Carry the learning agenda into the next wave and conduct a brief after-action review.

What I would bring

Disciplined instrument design, hands-on quantitative analysis, transparent QA, careful AI validation, concise writing, and the habit of turning ambiguous evidence into a decision-ready narrative. The lab is applied preparation—not a substitute for commercial tracking leadership.
