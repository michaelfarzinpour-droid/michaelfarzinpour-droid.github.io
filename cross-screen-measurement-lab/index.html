Interpretation guardrails

Selection: Households that receive ads can differ from others in baseline interest and viewing propensity.

Attribution: A difference in conversion rates does not show the ad caused the outcome.

Better design: Explore a randomized holdout or credible propensity-matched control, check covariate balance and observation windows, and estimate uncertainty.

Metric boundaries: Fictional tune-in represents a household-level viewing outcome, not ticket purchases or box-office revenue.

Planning assumptions & caveats

Each channel's projected unique-reach contribution scales with the square root of spend relative to its fictional baseline spend. A constant overlap penalty is applied to the projected summed reach. This is deliberately simplistic: real inventories, auctions, identities, audience intersections, diminishing returns, and reach curves require empirical modeling. The reach index is directional and has no statistical confidence interval.

      Study design and interpretation

      Estimand: difference in outcome proportions by randomized assignment, regardless of delivery (ITT).

      Interval: illustrative 95% normal-approximation CI for the unadjusted difference in independent proportions. It may perform poorly with rare outcomes or small samples.

      Design: baseline propensity measured before assignment; assignment independent of it; fixed observation window and one outcome per household.

      Guardrails: no interference, missingness, interference effects, or cross-device identity errors are modeled. This experiment is entirely separate from descriptive exposure results elsewhere in the lab.

      Methodology & transparency

      Data-generation process

      Three independent cohorts of 5,000 fictional households each are created using fixed-seed pseudorandom number generation. Each cohort includes an unobserved baseline interest score that influences channel exposure propensity and tune-in probability, intentionally demonstrating confounding.

      Each of three channel exposure counts is generated at the household level. A household is reached if its count is above zero, while total impressions are summed from exposure counts. Deduplicated reach is the number of unique households reached by at least one selected channel.

      Tune-in outcomes are fictional Bernoulli draws driven by baseline interest and a small modelled ad-associated component. The display does not identify that effect because exposed and unexposed groups are not exchangeable.

      Metric definitions

      Reach: Count of households with one or more selected-channel impressions.

      Frequency: Sum of selected-channel impressions / reached households.

      Duplicated channel reach: Sum of per-channel unique reach minus combined unique reach; this is a count of excess channel memberships, not simply the number reached by 2+ channels.

      Tune-in rate: Fictional households with the viewing outcome / households in that exposure group.

      Observed difference: Exposed rate minus unexposed rate in percentage points.

      Scope, uncertainty, and reproducibility

      Fixed seed makes results reproducible in this offline file. All figures are generated client-side; no external scripts, analytics, cookies, user accounts or API connections are used. The synthetic population is not a sample from real media audiences. The observational dashboard has no population weighting, confidence intervals, identity-error model, co-viewing adjustment, invalid traffic checks, geographic representation, or genuine experimental control. A separate randomized synthetic scenario provides illustrative ITT confidence intervals and baseline balance diagnostics; it is not linked to the observational exposures or real-world experimental evidence. The separate quality-control fixture is deliberately corrupted and is not a full production data pipeline. It is an analyst-workflow demonstration only.

      For industry context, see Samba's public site, CIMM's convergent TV measurement guide, and Google's public cross-media reach definitions. These references inform terminology only, not this project's data or algorithms.
