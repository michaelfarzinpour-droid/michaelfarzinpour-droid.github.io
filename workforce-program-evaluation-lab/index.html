State Opportunity & Skills Initiative

A fictional state agency operates short-term occupational training in four regions. It seeks to understand changes in employment and annualized earnings relative to eligible nonparticipants, and barriers requiring additional investigation.

480 participants and 480 eligible nonparticipants; before/after observations; enrollment was not randomized.

Interpretation safeguards

Comparison-adjusted changes require unverified parallel-trend and composition assumptions. One baseline wave does not establish counterfactual trends; selection bias, local labor markets and differential missingness remain threats. The lab reports descriptive estimates without p-values or confidence intervals.
