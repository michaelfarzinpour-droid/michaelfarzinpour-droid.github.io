Who needs this story?

Scenario: A hypothetical university wants to communicate the value of employer-informed education pathways for working adults. Choose a priority audience.

Employer partners: practical workforce pathways

Lead with skills relevance and the specific questions employers ask about talent development. Avoid inventing employment outcomes.

Measures to assess

Qualified partner inquiries, engaged reading time and click-through to relevant resources.

Guardrails

Verify graduation and employment claims against authoritative reporting; distinguish interviews from representative evidence; never present a composite learner as a real person.

Impact blog opening · Searchable explanatory feature

What makes an education pathway useful to working adults? Start with the demands competing for their time—and the skills employers need. Education stories can be told through promises or through observable decisions. A responsible institutional impact story should show which learner needs were identified, what partners contributed, and what evidence would demonstrate improvement. This example is a proposed editorial approach, not an account of a real university initiative.
