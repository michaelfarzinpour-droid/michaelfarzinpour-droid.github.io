METHODOLOGY APPENDIX

Make every metric and decision auditable.

These definitions, attribution assumptions, adjustment rules, and decision gates govern the synthetic analysis. They are illustrative—not retailer specifications or causal estimates.

SALESΣ net recognized retail sales

Gross merchandise value less cancellations and returns; taxes and shipping excluded.

UNITSΣ fulfilled units

Completed units sold after cancellations and returns.

DISTRIBUTIONCarrying-store category ACV ÷ total category ACV

Weighted availability; digital marketplaces use eligible assortment coverage.

RETAIL CONVERSIONOrders ÷ product-detail sessions

Media CVR is reported separately as attributed orders ÷ media clicks.

CATEGORY SHAREBrand sales ÷ category sales

Calculated within the same retailer, category, geography, and reporting period.

MEDIA EFFICIENCYROAS = attributed sales ÷ spend

CPC = spend ÷ clicks; CTR = clicks ÷ impressions; CVR = orders ÷ clicks.

INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY

Attributed sales are not automatically incremental sales.

ROAS describes retailer-attributed return under the stated windows. A causal lift claim would require a randomized holdout, geo experiment, matched-market design, or another credible counterfactual.

SEPARATING MEDIA FROM COMMERCIAL CONFOUNDERS

1 · Establish baseline

Compare retailer × SKU × week against an eight-week pre-period and the matched year-ago week.

2 · Availability

Remove weeks with material out-of-stocks; model changes in weighted distribution and eligible assortment.

3 · Price and promotion

Control for average selling price, discount depth, couponing, feature/display, and promotion timing.

4 · Seasonality

Account for holidays, retailer events, category seasonality, and common category-level shocks.

5 · Media contribution

Read the remaining media-associated lift against exposed/holdout or matched markets; otherwise label it descriptive, not causal.

Observed sales change= baseline + distribution + price/promotion + seasonality + media-associated residual
INVEST

Scale with guardrails

  • ROAS ≥ 4.0× for two weekly reads
  • CVR declines no more than 10% as spend rises
  • No material tracking, inventory, or distribution issue
  • Increase budget in 15–25% increments
TEST

Buy the next piece of evidence

  • ROAS 2.5–3.9× or promising segment lift
  • Attribution, creative, or audience uncertainty remains
  • Predefine KPI, control, four-week window, and $10K cap
  • Require ≥15% CVR lift to graduate
STOP

Pause or redesign

  • ROAS < 2.0× for two weekly reads after QA
  • Spend pace >110% without proportional sales
  • Broken tracking, severe overlap, or out-of-stock rate >10%
  • Document cause and restart criteria before relaunch

MY RECOMMENDED APPROACH

Manage the engagement as one decision system.

The work should move from aligned questions to controlled measurement, decisive optimization, and documented learning.

  1. 01

    Align the decision

    Define the business question, owner, decision date, KPI hierarchy, retailer constraints, and what action each possible finding would trigger.

  2. 02

    Build a reliable measurement plan

    Reconcile definitions across retailer portals, media partners, and internal reporting; document attribution windows, data latency, exclusions, and source-of-truth rules.

  3. 03

    Run workstreams visibly

    Maintain owners, milestones, dependencies, approvals, budget pacing, risks, and escalation triggers across insights, retail, media, creative, and agency partners.

  4. 04

    Interrogate the “why”

    Move from headline KPIs into retailer, product, audience, placement, and creative cuts; test whether weak results reflect measurement, availability, relevance, or conversion friction.

  5. 05

    Recommend with decision rules

    Separate invest, test, and stop recommendations; quantify expected tradeoffs, define guardrails, and state what evidence would change the recommendation.

  6. 06

    Close the learning loop

    Track implementation, compare realized outcomes with the hypothesis, capture lessons, and feed them into the next retail and creative planning cycle.
