Streaming Survey Operations

A reproducible example of survey export QA, recruiting quota monitoring, SQL summaries, and automated reporting.

All data are generated. No people were surveyed or recruited for this project. The two fictional promotion concepts describe the same imagined streaming series: Character-led emphasizes relationships; Action-led emphasizes suspense. No actual trailer was shown.

Fielding review

QA retained 576 of 656 exported rows and excluded 80. The fielding goal is 600 valid completes. The age quotas require 25 additional valid completes in the open segments shown below. Use quota deficits to direct additional recruitment before closing the mock study.
