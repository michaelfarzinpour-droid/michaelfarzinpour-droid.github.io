03

Equity lens

Participation share versus enrollment share.

The gap below is descriptive: a group’s share of reported AP participants minus its share of total enrollment. It surfaces questions; it does not identify causes.

How to read it

Left of zero means the group makes up a smaller share of reported AP participants than of enrollment.

Right of zero means the group makes up a larger share.

Reported sex-component counts are summed only when nonnegative. Unavailable or suppressed components can understate totals.

04

Model & robustness

Associations, not causal effects.

A school-level logistic regression uses state-cluster bootstrap intervals. Continuous predictors are standardized, so each odds ratio reflects a one-standard-deviation difference.

05

Transparent pipeline

Every exclusion and join stays visible.

COMBOKEY and NCESSCH are standardized as 12-character identifiers. CRDC files join one-to-one; the CRDC-to-CCD join is left-sided so unmatched records remain auditable.

Universe

Schools matched to CCD and marked as offering grade 12. The primary outcome is at least one reported AP course.

Special values

Negative CRDC reserve codes are not counts. Within the grade-12 universe, −9 is treated as no AP offering; exceptional codes remain missing.

Uncertainty

Access rates include Wilson 95% confidence intervals. Model uncertainty comes from 150 bootstrap replications clustered by state.

Guardrail

The dashboard is a screening and inquiry tool—not a school ranking or a causal evaluation of AP.

06

My recommended approach

Validate. Diagnose. Co-design. Monitor.

The analysis is most useful when it initiates a disciplined conversation with district partners rather than substituting for one.

  1. 1

    Validate the signal

    Confirm IDs, definitions, suppression rules, confidence intervals, course catalogs, and local records.

  2. 2

    Diagnose the opportunity

    Examine subjects, seats, staffing, prerequisites, scheduling, counseling, demand, and alternative pathways.

  3. 3

    Co-design a response

    Define the decision, feasible intervention, owner, timeline, and equity guardrails with stakeholders.

  4. 4

    Monitor and learn

    Predefine leading measures, review dates, and stop / adjust / scale rules before implementation.
