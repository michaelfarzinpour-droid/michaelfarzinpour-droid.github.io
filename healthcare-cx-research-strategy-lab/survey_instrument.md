# Draft CX Survey Instrument — Illustrative

**Independent portfolio simulation; not fielded.**

Intro: We are studying your organization's experience with ordering and support. Do not include patient information. Participation is voluntary.

1. Which best describes your organization? Independent pharmacy / Regional pharmacy group / Specialty clinic / Health system / Other / Prefer not to say.
2. Which of the following interactions have you completed in the last 90 days? Ordering / Shipment tracking / Support / Returns or credits (select all).
3. Thinking about your most recent interaction, how satisfied were you overall? 1 Very dissatisfied to 5 Very satisfied; Not applicable.
4. How easy was it to complete your task? 1 Very difficult to 7 Very easy; Not applicable.
5. How likely are you to recommend this provider to a peer organization? 0–10; Prefer not to say. [Exploratory recommendation metric, not a validated organizational loyalty outcome.]
6. Did you encounter an ordering portal problem in the last 90 days? Yes / No / Don't know / Did not use portal.
7. Was your most recent delivery later than the communicated expected time? Yes / No / Don't know / Not applicable.
8. If you contacted support about your most recent issue, was it resolved in your first contact? Yes / No / Didn't contact / Don't know.
9. Which step required the most effort? Ordering / Shipment tracking / Account support / Returns or credits / None / Other.
10. What single change would most improve your experience? [Open text; request no patient or confidential details.]

Programming: eligibility & duplicate rules, randomized non-scaled option ordering where appropriate, neutral wording, mobile QA, accessibility review, pilot cognitive interviews, suppressed recontacts, and reportable segment cell thresholds.
