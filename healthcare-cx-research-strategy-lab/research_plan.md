# Healthcare Customer Experience Research & Strategy Lab — Research Plan

**Independent portfolio simulation. Fictional business, synthetic data, fabricated interview themes. Not affiliated with Cencora.**

## Business decision
What should a fictional healthcare distributor prioritize in its next 90-day customer-experience improvement cycle: ordering portal usability, delivery-status visibility, support handoffs, or returns/credits transparency?

## Learning objectives
1. Locate friction across customer segments and journey stages.
2. Test associations of portal problems, delivery delays, and first-contact resolution with CSAT and perceived ease.
3. Explore why customers experience friction and which unmet needs matter.
4. Recommend a feasible pilot with owners, leading and lagging indicators, and an evaluation plan.

## Methods and sequencing
**Phase 0 — Intake:** align decision, definitions, customer segments, constraints, and success measures with Operations, Product, Account Support, Marketing, and Compliance.
**Phase 1 — Exploratory:** ~12–20 semi-structured interviews with purposive segment/stage diversity; do not treat qualitative frequency as prevalence.
**Phase 2 — Measurement:** survey ~400–600 eligible customer contacts, with a sampling frame, eligibility/suppression rules, quotas, and response/nonresponse review. This demo provides 600 synthetic records, not an actual sample.
**Phase 3 — Triangulation:** compare survey measures with de-identified operational indicators, examine subgroup uncertainty, and validate interpretive consistency using a documented qualitative codebook.
**Phase 4 — Action:** co-design a focused 90-day pilot, document tradeoffs, collect baseline measures, and evaluate against a pre-specified comparison wherever feasible.

## Bias, privacy, and validity
Recruit beyond dissatisfied customers. Define customer organization vs contact as units of analysis. Check duplicates, missingness, mode/device effects, contact suppression, response mix, and subgroup sample sizes. Avoid recording protected health information. Account for nonresponse and segment imbalance; avoid inferring causation from observational differences. Seek legal/compliance review before live use.

## Illustrative timeline
Weeks 1–2 intake, governance, and instruments; 3–4 qualitative discovery; 5–7 survey and operational extraction; 8–9 synthesis and validation; 10 decision workshop; 11–12 executive readout and pilot handoff.

## Limitations
Synthetic records were deliberately generated with specified associations. They are not representative of any real healthcare distributor and do not provide estimates of Cencora's customers or operations.
