# Executive Decision Brief — Illustrative

**Healthcare Customer Experience Research & Strategy Lab**
**Independent synthetic simulation; no affiliation with Cencora.**

## Decision
Prioritize a 90-day pilot focused on reducing digital ordering friction and improving exception communications, subject to stakeholder validation and implementation feasibility. This is an illustrative recommendation based on intentionally generated records, not a finding about any real company.

## Evidence model
Triangulate survey satisfaction and effort, service/fulfillment metrics, and exploratory interviews. Compare patterns by customer segment. Investigate operational confounding and response mix. Seek contradictory cases before escalating any recommendation.

## Proposed 90-day action
Days 0–30: map workflow, baseline portal issue and shipment exception rates, validate accessibility and support handoffs.
Days 31–60: pilot revised error recovery and proactive exception messaging with an agreed control/comparison design where operationally feasible.
Days 61–90: review task completion, customer effort, first-contact resolution, complaint volume, and operational guardrails; decide scale/iterate/stop.

## Success and safeguards
Primary: successful order completion and customer-reported ease among eligible users. Secondary: contact rate and first-contact resolution. Guardrails: fulfillment accuracy, safety/privacy, accessibility, and differential effects by segment. Report confidence/uncertainty and operational costs. Avoid overclaiming causality.

## Stakeholder asks
Product: feasibility and instrumentation. Operations: delivery-status definitions. Support: contact categorization and escalation paths. Compliance: privacy and communication review. CX lead: decision owner and pilot exit criteria.
