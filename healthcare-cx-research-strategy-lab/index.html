Qualitative triangulation: invented themes

Six illustrative themes were distributed across 24 fabricated entries: duplicate entry, error recovery, visibility gap, exception alerts, handoff friction, and resolution opacity. These are not real customer quotes. They illustrate how one might pair a codebook and journey observations with survey patterns.

A real study would seek disconfirming examples, review coding disagreements, and avoid treating qualitative frequencies as prevalence.

Applies to the segment/region filters above. Confidence intervals are approximate 95% intervals for the unweighted synthetic records. The regression is illustrative OLS, not a causal model, and assumes independent contact records.

Proposed measurement strategy

Primary: successful order completion, perceived ease (1–7), and customer-reported friction.

Secondary: repeat support contacts, first-contact resolution, visibility of exceptions, and CSAT (1–5).

Guardrails: fulfillment accuracy, accessibility, privacy, complaint escalation, and segment disparities.

Interpretation: baseline survey associations do not prove intervention effects. Pre-specify a pilot comparison design wherever feasible.
