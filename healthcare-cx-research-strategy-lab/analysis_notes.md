# Analysis and Interpretation Notes

Data are generated by a controlled simulation, not collected from customers. Variables include segment, region, portal issue, delivery delay, first-contact resolution, CSAT (1–5), perceived ease (1–7; higher is easier), 0–10 recommendation item, and primary friction stage.

Recommended analyses: eligible denominators and missingness, distribution and descriptive subgroup summaries, cross-tabulation, exploratory uncertainty intervals, regression with pre-specified covariates if justified by sample and assumptions, and sensitivity to response and selection bias.

Important: differences shown in the lab are descriptive associations in synthetic data. Generating rules intentionally influence CSAT and ease. Do not present observed patterns as causal, statistically generalizable, real customer evidence, or verified business impact. A controlled operational pilot with guardrails could strengthen later causal inference.
