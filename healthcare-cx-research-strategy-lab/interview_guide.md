# Semi-structured Customer Interview Guide — Illustrative

**Simulation only; no interviews conducted.** Target: 30–45 minutes. Seek consent and appropriate confidentiality controls before any live study.

Opening: Describe your responsibilities and the ordering/delivery workflow you use. What did you last need to accomplish?

Journey walkthrough: Walk through your most recent task from initial need to completion. Where did you switch systems, seek help, or wait? What was your expectation at each step?

Critical incident: Describe a recent situation that required extra effort. What happened before, during, and after? How was it resolved?

Probe without leading: What information was missing? How did you decide what to do next? Who else was involved? What alternative did you consider?

Outcome & prioritization: What consequences did that friction create for your team? If one step changed, what would improve first? What tradeoffs would be acceptable?

Closing: What did we fail to ask? May we follow up for clarification?

Analysis: Two researchers pilot a codebook; record negative cases; separate respondent language from analyst inference; do not treat 24 fabricated interview records as real evidence.
