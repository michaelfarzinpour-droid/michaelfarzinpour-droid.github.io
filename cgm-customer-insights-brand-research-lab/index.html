Methods and transparency

How the simulation works

01

Design

Fictional, multi-market brand tracker with synthetic patient and HCP samples, paired with synthetic depth-interview themes and digital feedback.

02

Quality

Eligibility screeners, soft quotas, logic tests, duplicate and speed checks, minimum-base flags, documented exclusions, and source-level review.

03

Analysis

Descriptive comparisons, funnel diagnostics, message-score contrasts, rule-based qualitative coding, and AI-to-human validation.

04

Limits

Synthetic results demonstrate research thinking, not product performance, market facts, clinical evidence, or causal effects.

Open the research brief and sample specifications

Patient tracker

n=720: U.S. 360, U.K. 180, Germany 180. Soft quotas by diabetes type, insulin status, age, and CGM experience.

HCP tracker

n=180: endocrinologists, primary-care clinicians, diabetes educators, and prescribing nurses.

Qualitative layer

30 synthetic depth interviews, 240 synthetic feedback records, and a six-theme codebook with inclusion and exclusion rules.

Reporting rule

Flag bases below 50, separate observed patterns from explanations, and label all recommendations as hypotheses to test.
