# Fictional Service Routing & Decision Log

**Purpose:** Demonstrate how an analyst documents fit-for-purpose options, without granting technical access or regulatory authorization.

| ID | Request | Considered options | Suggested next consultation | Rationale | Pending decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Initial cohort feasibility | Aggregate counts vs record-level extract | Data/feasibility service owner | Counts may address initial feasibility with lower data granularity | Confirm approved tool and constraints |
| D-02 | Follow-up questionnaires | Supported EDC vs ad hoc spreadsheets | Data capture service owner (e.g. REDCap consultation) | Roles, collection rules, auditability, and training need review | Configuration/access |
| D-03 | Analyze longitudinal records | Personal cloud vs approved institutional solution | Research computing, security, data owner | Patient-level dataset placement requires authorized review | Environment and access determination |
| D-04 | External collaborator needs records | Unreviewed transfer vs governance referral | Privacy, compliance, data owner, security | IRB status is not a blanket data-transfer authorization | Permitted use and transfer |

No option in this fictional record constitutes approval to access or share data.
