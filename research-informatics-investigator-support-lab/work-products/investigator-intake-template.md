## Core discovery questions
1. What research question and decision will the result support?
2. What population, time window, units of analysis, and fields are needed?
3. Are counts sufficient, or is record-level information needed?
4. Who may view, transfer, store, or export the data, subject to institutional authorization?
5. Which dependencies, deadlines, funding/compute limits, and support needs exist?

**Do not collect real protected health information in this sample template.**
