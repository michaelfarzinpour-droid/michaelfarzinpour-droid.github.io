# Research Support KPI Dictionary & Improvement Plan (Synthetic)

**Population:** 60 deterministic fictional service tickets. Not UCLA operational data. No empirical effect estimates or claims of actual service outcomes.

| KPI | Numerator/measure | Denominator | Interpretation / guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand volume | Count of received tickets in period | N/A | Not patient or study volume |
| Median first substantive response | Median days from request receipt to actionable human response | Tickets with response value | Not automatic acknowledgment; not a data-delivery clock |
| Closed share | Tickets marked Closed | Tickets in filter | Closure can include no-access outcomes; check closure definitions |
| Open backlog | Tickets not Closed | Tickets in filter | Snapshot depends on age and case mix |
| Blocker flag share | Tickets with documented blocker | Tickets in filter | Detection practice affects count |
| Median elapsed days | Median snapshot elapsed days | Tickets in filter | Do not confuse open age with completed turnaround |

## Illustrative improvement hypothesis
A structured intake checklist and named handoff coordinator could reduce avoidable clarification loops and ownership delays. This is a **hypothesis**, not a finding.

## Four-week example pilot
1. Establish baseline with definitions and transparent service pathway mix.
2. Introduce a short intake checklist and triage ownership rules to one fictional workstream.
3. Monitor weekly volume, completeness, substantive response, pending decisions, elapsed days, and requester feedback.
4. Examine case mix and unintended delays; do not make causal claims from an uncontrolled before/after comparison.
5. Review whether to adapt, stop, or expand with appropriate service and governance leads.

## Monitoring safeguards
- Display denominators and avoid interpreting n<5 segments as stable evidence.
- Do not equate referral with fulfillment or acknowledgment with approval.
- Protect confidentiality and report aggregate service metrics only.
- Technical and regulatory specialists retain their decision authority.
