The operating model

An investigator's need is more than a technology request.

A good research informatics service partner defines the actual outcome, clarifies constraints, routes to the right experts, and documents follow-through without claiming authority they do not hold.

1. Discovery before solutioning

Clarify hypothesis, cohort, unit of analysis, data elements, timing, approvals, and whether the task needs counts, a limited dataset, or a secure analysis environment.

Requirements

2. Fit-for-purpose routing

Distinguish feasibility queries, data acquisition, electronic data capture, and computing/storage support. Engage specialists instead of assuming one platform can satisfy every request.

Service selection

3. Governance without shortcuts

Identify issues involving sensitive data, authorization, research purpose, access, retention, and transfer. Refer decisions to appropriate compliance, privacy, security, or data custodians.

Data stewardship

4. Operational visibility

Track request status, dependencies, ownership, follow-up, and turnaround using transparent definitions and synthetic metrics—not fabricated operational outcomes.

Methods, boundaries, and sources

What the simulation evaluates

Requirements completeness, service-routing judgment, appropriate escalation, record continuity, responsible data use, and interpretation of service metrics. The 12 decision questions use a designer-defined preferred answer, not a validated clinical competency assessment.

Every decision receives explanatory feedback. A choice may be suboptimal without being inherently prohibited. Actual governance depends on local policies and specialist review.

Dataset design

60 deterministic fictional tickets across four pathways. Response days, elapsed days, statuses, and blocker flags follow illustrative patterns, not a statistical model calibrated to UCLA. Median is used to reduce sensitivity to skew; sample sizes accompany every filter.

Tickets do not include real patient records, protected health information, credentials, or institution-specific operational data.
