Interpretation safeguards

  • Every record is deterministic synthetic data.
  • Wilson 95% intervals describe binomial sampling uncertainty among observed responses; they do not correct selection or nonresponse bias.
  • Milestone completion is observed only for follow-up respondents; rates are not equivalent to program impact.
  • No identification, random assignment, or counterfactual is present.
  • Investigate data quality and qualitative explanations before expanding a program.

Analysis protocol

Document sample flow and consent assumptions; verify unique IDs and eligible denominators; audit follow-up by program and recruitment channel; characterize baseline scores; quantify observed rates and uncertainty; conduct missing-outcome sensitivity analysis; code interviews using inclusion/exclusion rules; review negative cases; and present qualified action options.

Fictional survey questions and interview excerpts illustrate instrument design, not actual validated measures. Construct validity, translations, accessibility, and consent would require real-world pilot testing.

Learning cycle and governance

Roles and escalation

Evaluation lead: defines standards, checks claims and coordinates milestones. Program leads: own instruments and follow-through. Community advisors: review relevance and burden. Data steward: checks access, consent, retention, and small cells. Executive sponsor: decides on action and resource approvals.

Illustrative RACI-type responsibilities only; not a depiction of UCLA staffing.
