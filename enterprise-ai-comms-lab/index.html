Scenario

Meridian Relay is preparing a release of Review Assist, an optional AI feature that summarizes approved project notes and surfaces unresolved review items. A trade reporter asks whether it makes decisions autonomously. A separate social post incorrectly claims the feature trains on all customer records.

Your mandate: coordinate Product, Legal/Policy, People, and Communications; publish only verified claims; adapt language without changing meaning; and measure what audiences actually do.

Operating principles

  1. Confirm facts with the accountable subject-matter expert.
  2. Distinguish pilot observations from general claims.
  3. Protect sensitive details and route policy assertions for review.
  4. Record approvals and dependencies before publication.
  5. Use performance evidence to improve, not exaggerate, impact.

09:00 media scan and triage · 10:00 editorial stand-up · 11:00 product verification · 13:00 cross-functional review · 15:00 approved channel adaptation · 16:30 status and next-day planning.

10 / operational readiness

30 / 60 / 90-day communications plan

Days 1–30 · Establish

Inventory channels and claims; establish daily triage, review SLAs, risk register and baseline metrics. Interview Product, Policy, People and executives.

Deliverable: governance map and 30-day calendar.

Days 31–60 · Execute

Deliver weekly releases and bylines; rehearse rapid response and review approvals. Introduce a controlled headline test and lessons-learned log.

Deliverable: weekly executive report and tested playbook.

Days 61–90 · Improve

Compare content within channels, monitor review cycle time, and adjust publishing rhythm. Validate any improvement against baseline and audience mix.

Deliverable: quarterly recommendations and revised calendar.
