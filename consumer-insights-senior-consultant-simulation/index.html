Coaching conversation

Situation: Analyst B claims rising awareness proves an ad campaign worked.

Feedback: Rewrite it as a descriptive trend, identify alternatives such as sampling or distribution changes, and propose a controlled test.

Research engagement structure

Proposed methodology

Four independent tracking waves; synthetic n=600/wave; quota monitoring by age and region; five brand indicators; logic and speed checks; Wilson intervals; explicit exclusion documentation; targeted proposed qualitative interviews.

Executive recommendation

Do not redirect creative investment based solely on descriptive tracker changes. Investigate the awareness-to-consideration conversion gap, test audience and regional heterogeneity, and run a randomized creative test before reallocation.
