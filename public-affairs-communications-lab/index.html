The assignment

A fictional research team has completed a study about access to neighborhood cooling resources. Your job is to make the findings understandable, accurate, and useful across institutional channels.

Fictional research dossier

SIMULATED STUDY — NOT ACTUAL FINDINGS

Cooling Access & Urban Neighborhoods

A fabricated cross-sectional survey explores the relationship between neighborhood information access and awareness of public cooling facilities. The scenario is deliberately simple so the focus stays on responsible editorial communication.

600 fictional residentsIllustrative survey responses across six imaginary neighborhoods
64% versus 39%Illustrative cooling-center awareness among respondents who had versus had not received neighborhood alerts
25-percentage-point differenceAn association in fabricated data, not evidence that alerts caused higher awareness
Survey-based; noncausalConvenience sample; recall bias; no representative population inference
