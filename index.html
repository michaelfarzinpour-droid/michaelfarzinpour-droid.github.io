Film marketplace case study

Film demand beyond
opening weekend.

Elio opened 30.5% below 28 Years Later, yet earned 46.2% more in weekend four and 18.2% more per theater. A smaller opening can still deserve a closer look.

This study compares five selected June 2025 releases using public box office data and two official YouTube trailer snapshots. The recommendation is to include later demand alongside opening size in a client pitch, then research audience fit before brand or creator outreach.

What to include in a client pitch

Present opening size and week-four retention together. Elio’s fourth-weekend performance gives an agent useful context that its smaller opening alone would miss. The two films share a release date but differ in genre and distribution.

Brand and creator research

F1 had the highest fourth-weekend revenue per theater in this case study: $3,192. Before a film and sports brand pitch, check audience overlap and brand affinity to select creators or sponsors.

Before selecting creators or partners

For an F1 film and sports idea, request recent organic viewing, audience geography, and overlap with the brand’s customers. Use those measures to qualify a creator or sponsor prospect.

What the counts leave unanswered

Views can include repeat plays and paid distribution. These records do not show unique viewers, watch time, demographics, sentiment, conversion or monetization. Regional uploads and other trailers are excluded.

Methods and sources

A reproducible public data study

Data and calculations

Twenty recorded rows cover the first four reported weekends for five selected wide June 2025 releases. This case study does not represent the full market.

Python and SQLite independently calculate revenue per theater and retention. The Excel workbook recalculates the same metrics from exact gross and theater counts, rather than the publisher’s rounded averages.

Source checks cover unique film/week keys, complete date sequences, positive venue counts, and agreement with published averages and weekly changes.

Download data and analysisDownload the source rows

Interpretation

Opening figures retain published preview conventions. Holiday timing, genre, competition and distribution can affect the comparison.

Per-theater revenue does not control for ticket prices, premium formats, showtimes, seats or the changing mix of venues. Box office does not identify demographics, marketing effects or profitability.

The two YouTube records are a separate layer with different timing and geography. They cannot explain or predict the 2025 box office results. No TikTok, Instagram or Facebook performance data were collected.
