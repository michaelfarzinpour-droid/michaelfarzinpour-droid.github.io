Peer benchmarking and comparability audit

Six fictional campuses. Comparable reporting requires aligned belonging item, survey period, scoring, and eligible population. Toggle exclusions to see why a simple ranking is misleading.

Confidence intervals are approximate binomial intervals conditional on reported valid n; synthetic rates and sample sizes were specified for the exercise. No significance claims across campuses.

Confidentiality and data stewardship

A publication-ready university dashboard requires data-owner sign-off, access review, secondary-suppression assessment, FERPA/privacy review where applicable, versioned metric definitions, and logging of every new export.
