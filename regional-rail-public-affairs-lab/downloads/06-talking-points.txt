COMMUNITY MEETING TALKING POINTS — FICTIONAL EXERCISE

1. Start with project purpose and acknowledged impacts, not engineering detail.
2. Separate confirmed work from tentative closures.
3. Explain noise, access, school-route, and habitat controls.
4. State who owns each concern and when the community will hear back.
5. Record unanswered questions; do not improvise facts.
6. Close with contact channels, the next milestone, and follow-through commitments.
