MEDIA RESPONSE — FICTIONAL EXERCISE

QUESTION
Why did customers hear about the disruption on social media before the agency issued an alert?

RESPONSE
Customers deserved a faster acknowledgement. Our first priority was confirming which segments were affected so we did not amplify an inaccurate systemwide-closure claim. Once operations verified the service area, we issued a bounded update that identified the affected locations, customer actions, and the time of the next update. We are reviewing the first-alert workflow so an acknowledgement can be issued sooner while operational details are still being confirmed.

RESPONSE STRUCTURE
1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. State only verified facts.
3. Explain the immediate customer action.
4. Commit to the next update or corrective step.
