08 · Voice of Member operations

Reliable insight starts upstream.

The operating design covers survey lifecycle controls, reporting integrity, and human validation of AI-assisted text analytics.

Survey configuration

Trigger and eligibility

  • Transactional invite 24 hours after eligible journey event
  • Member contactability and consent checked before distribution
  • 30-day journey-level and 14-day enterprise suppression
  • Instrument version and trigger source written to every record
Quality assurance

Pre- and post-launch

  • Link, logic, device, piping, and data-capture tests
  • Invitation-to-response feed reconciliation
  • Duplicate, speed, straight-line, and impossible-value flags
  • Documented exception disposition and audit trail
Reporting consistency

Controlled definitions

  • Versioned KPI dictionary and calculation owner
  • Stable segment denominators and minimum base sizes
  • Restatement log when logic changes
  • Monthly refresh checklist with named sign-off
AI-assisted text review

Methodology appendix
How are the core KPIs calculated?

NPS = % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6)   MSAT = valid mean on a 1–5 scale   MES = valid mean effort on a 1–7 scale; lower is better   Response rate = completes ÷ eligible delivered invitations

What prevents false signals?

Movement is investigated only when the eligible base is at least 75, the absolute NPS change is at least 5 points or exceeds the rolling control limit, and the result survives checks for response mix, journey volume, instrument version, distribution errors, suppression failures, and data-feed reconciliation. A flag is a prompt to investigate—not a causal conclusion.

How are forecasting and uncertainty handled?

The forecast demonstration uses the synthetic monthly KPI series, reports 95% prediction intervals that widen with horizon, and separates observed values from forecasts. Significance labels are based on a difference test or interval around the change—not on whether two point estimates appear different.

How is intervention effectiveness estimated?

The checklist pilot uses a synthetic pre/post comparison with a contemporaneous holdout. The reported effect is the pilot group's change minus the comparison group's change, with a 95% confidence interval. The result is illustrative and does not establish performance at any real institution.

How are missing and invalid responses handled?

Missing item responses remain null and are excluded only from that metric's denominator. Records failing duplicate, impossible-value, or minimum-completion checks are retained in an exception log but excluded from reported KPIs. Results with bases below 30 are suppressed; bases from 30–74 are directional.

How is AI-assisted text analysis governed?

A fixed taxonomy defines inclusion and exclusion rules. A human-coded validation sample is compared with AI suggestions using percent agreement and class-level precision/recall. Disagreements are adjudicated, taxonomy changes are versioned, and quotes are reviewed before use. AI output never becomes a reported theme without validation.

How should the predictive risk score be used?

The risk model is a transparent synthetic demonstration for prioritizing investigation. It is not used to determine eligibility, pricing, credit, service access, or adverse action. A real implementation would require calibration, fairness testing, monitoring, model governance, and human review.
