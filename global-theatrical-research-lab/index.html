Transparency & reproducibility

Methodology and limitations

Study design (fictional)

Five hypothetical markets × 480 synthetic respondents = 2,400 invented records. The segment metrics, tracking waves, and creative cell outcomes are scenario tables authored for teaching; the CSV export contains clearly labeled market-by-segment summary rows, not respondent-level observations.

Creative comparison uses synthetic cell sizes of 240 per arm per market and an illustrative independent-proportions standard error. This approximates uncertainty under assumptions of independent Bernoulli samples, ignoring survey design, weighting, multiplicity, and any recruitment biases.

Research safeguards

The tool does not use real studio, theatrical-release, survey, vendor, or financial data. Numbers do not originate from a real study. Segment differences may reflect constructed assumptions rather than true audience patterns.

Recommended real-world validation: sampling plan and power analysis, weighting, translation and measurement invariance, creative exposure fidelity, prespecified outcomes, uncertainty intervals, longitudinal comparability, and integration with verified box-office comps.

Scope: This independent interactive lab is a skills demonstration, not commissioned research, a prediction, a vendor-managed engagement, or evidence of successful release decisions. No affiliation with Sony Pictures Entertainment is implied.
