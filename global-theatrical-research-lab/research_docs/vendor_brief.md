# Illustrative vendor brief and research-operations plan

**Client:** Fictional Project Horizon Global Research Team. **Status:** Practice brief, not issued to vendors. **Owner:** Illustrative global research manager.

## Scope and deliverables
- Tracking vendor A ($85,000 scenario allocation): four independent waves, five markets, cross-market harmonized tables, top-line each wave, data-quality report and localization sign-offs.
- Creative testing vendor B ($55,000): A/B trailer and poster test plan, balanced assignment, research stimulus QC, market-specific results and confidence intervals.
- Qualitative vendor C ($30,000): two proposed groups per market, discussion guide, translated transcripts where needed, coded themes, contradictory evidence and implications.
- Internal reporting ($15,000): weekly executive readout, action log, reporting template and dashboard QA.
- **Illustrative total:** $185,000. User-selected decision-lab add-on of $18,000 or $12,000 changes the hypothetical total to $203,000 or $197,000; no quote or spend actually occurred.

## Vendor evaluation matrix (proposed)
Score methods (30%), localization and market expertise (20%), data QA (20%), timelines (15%), cost and transparency (15%). Require documented sampling partner, panel screening, translation process, full methodology, data dictionary, de-identified records and revision policy.

## Timeline and gates
T−8 baseline / scope; T−6 creative fielding; T−5 creative top-line; T−4 qualitative diagnosis and re-test decision; T−2 tracker and executive recommendation. Gate: stimulus translation and local pretest; randomization/exposure QA; moderator guide review; QC issue log and executive sign-off.

## Escalation examples
- Localization issue: hold affected comparative readout until corrected, document affected markets, obtain local approval.
- Seven-day delay: adjust preliminary readout, explain missing markets and revised decision dates.
- Fourteen-day delay: escalate if release decision window is threatened and propose phased delivery.
- High creative uncertainty: favor additional research over claiming a global win.

## Reporting template
Business decision | Market | Study/wave | Sample and limitations | Effect size + interval | Qualitative diagnostic | Recommendation | Owner | Decision deadline | Risk status.
