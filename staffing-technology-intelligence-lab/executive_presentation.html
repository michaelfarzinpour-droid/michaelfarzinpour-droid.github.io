Screening risk

False positives versus missed fraud

  • Threshold affects sensitivity and specificity
  • Precision changes with prevalence
  • Human review, recourse and cost must be measured
  • Model parameters are invented; no real classifier is assessed

Executive recommendation

Pilot, verify, then scale

  • Choose a measurable operational bottleneck
  • Separate observed evidence, attributed claims, and hypothetical models
  • Require cost, quality, fairness and human-review guardrails
  • Reassess when new evidence changes assumptions
