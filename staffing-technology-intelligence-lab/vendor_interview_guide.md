# Technology Provider and Staffing Executive Interview Guides

**Independent portfolio exercise — no interviews were conducted.**

## Comparable technology provider briefing (30 minutes)
1. Product boundaries: which steps are natively supported, subcontracted or connected via API? Live-demo a complete use case.
2. Data definitions: report customer counts, installed base versus trials, and eligible feature users; specify regions/verticals.
3. Outcomes: define the numerator, denominator and evaluation period for claimed time savings, placements, retention and error reduction.
4. Evidence design: request baseline distribution and comparison group, exclusion rules, selection bias, missingness, attrition, confidence intervals, evaluator independence.
5. Operations: demonstrate failure modes, exception handling, downtime, versioning, ATS/CRM reconciliation and export.
6. AI safety: request data provenance, drift monitoring, privacy/security controls, subgroup impact evaluation, human appeal and accessibility options.
7. Economics: subscription tiers, per-use fees, implementation, training, data storage, termination/portability and expected total cost.
8. Reference checks: speak with customers selected by both vendor and researcher; document nonresponse.

## Staffing executive interview (30 minutes)
1. Which recruiting or billing workflow is your binding constraint?
2. What was the baseline and reason for selecting this vendor?
3. What changed in data definitions or case mix after launch?
4. What integration or training difficulty had the largest operational impact?
5. What have recruiters, clients and candidates said (including complaints)?
6. What costs are not visible in the contract price?
7. Which decision would you reverse today, and why?
8. What would count as success six months from now?

## Claim audit example
**Fictional claim:** “Our AI sourcing engine improves placements by 35%.”
- Clarify absolute/relative denominator, placement definition, who was included, observation period, and how the result was measured.
- Ask for a comparable control/holdout and whether teams self-selected into adoption; separate marketing evidence from verified evidence.
- Assess baseline volume, seasonality, recruiter tenure, contract mix, excluded pilot failures and confidence intervals.
- Check for downstream harm: applicant access, incorrect rejections, complaints and human review.
- Analyst classification: 'verified performance' only after sufficient independent evidence; otherwise clearly attribute as vendor claim or exclude from findings.
