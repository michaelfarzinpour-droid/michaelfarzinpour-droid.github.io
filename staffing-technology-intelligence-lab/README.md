# Staffing Technology Intelligence Lab — Expanded Research Work Sample

Independent portfolio project | November 2026 | **Not affiliated with Staffing Industry Analysts**.

## Quick start
Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No server, package manager or external JS dependencies required. For **downloads to work reliably** in browsers restricting `file://` links, run `python -m http.server 8000` in this folder and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Ten interactive sections
1. Overview and separately labeled, sourced context
2. Synthetic survey explorer (360 deterministic fictional firm records; CSV export)
3. Hypothetical fraud-screening tradeoffs, cost and confusion matrix
4. Hypothetical staffing software spend / market-sizing sensitivity
5. Fictional vendor weighted comparison
6. **Expanded technology landscape**: six categories, buyer needs, commercial models, actual product names as non-rated examples, proof and risk questions
7. **Prospective survey protocol** with sample-size planning, questionnaire, eligibility, fielding, quality, weighting and inference notes
8. **Vendor and staffing executive interview guides** with interactive claim-verification challenge
9. **Interactive 12-slide briefing** with speaker notes and anticipated client questions
10. Executive research brief, limitations, source list and text export

## Supporting files
- `research_protocol.md`: complete prospective study design and caveats (not fielded)
- `vendor_interview_guide.md`: comparative briefing and claim-audit protocol
- `executive_presentation.html`: 12 printable slides with speaker notes on screen; use Print to PDF
- `data_dictionary.csv`: fields and meanings for 360-row fictional survey
- `reproduce_survey.py`: reproducible Python implementation of the JavaScript generator; creates CSV without third-party packages
- `index.html`: self-contained browser application

## Evidence policy
All surveys, respondent details, vendor scores, hypothetical finances and screening curves are **invented**. Published third-party data are clearly attributed, and product names are examples of categories, not tested or endorsed. No real interviews were conducted. Do not represent modeled figures as empirical staffing-industry estimates. The research protocol is proposed; no probability sampling, fieldwork or respondent-reported adoption was performed.
