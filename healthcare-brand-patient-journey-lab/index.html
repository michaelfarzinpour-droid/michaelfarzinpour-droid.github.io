04 · Research design

Decision-led methods

The design begins with the business decision, then assigns each method a distinct job. Every output includes limitations and a rule for what the evidence can support.

Business questionWhy does brand awareness not consistently translate into patient-initiated discussion or HCP recommendation?
QuantitativeOnline tracker: 420 patients and 120 HCPs across the US, UK, and Germany; market-level quotas; descriptive comparisons and trend signals.
Qualitative30 depth interviews: 18 patients and 12 HCPs; semi-structured guide; framework analysis with explicit inclusion/exclusion rules.
IntegrationJoint display connects funnel loss points with interview explanations, then separates supported findings from hypotheses requiring follow-up.
Q1 · Unaided awarenessWhich prescription treatments for chronic migraine, if any, have you heard of? [Open text; randomize coding review]
Q7 · ConsiderationHow likely are you to ask a healthcare professional about each treatment in the next six months? [5-point scale; randomized brands]
Q12 · Attribute batteryWhich treatment is most associated with clear benefits, manageable trade-offs, and fit with daily life? [Randomized items]
Quality controlsEligibility screener, duplicate check, minimum completion-time flag, straight-line review, and market quota monitoring.
Warm-upTell me about the path from first symptoms to your current approach to managing migraine.
Journey probeThink about your most recent treatment conversation. What made it easier—or harder—to raise a new option?
Stimulus exerciseReview three fictional message concepts. What feels clear, credible, relevant, or overstated? Why?
ClosingIf the brand team could change one thing about how this option is explained, what should it be?
ValidityTriangulate funnel measures with interview themes; inspect contradictory cases before finalizing the narrative.
ComparabilityUse translation/back-translation in real multi-market work; confirm cultural and healthcare-system meaning before combining markets.
LimitationsSynthetic, non-probability sample; directional comparisons only; no clinical-effectiveness or causal claims.
PrivacyA real study would require consent, secure handling, restricted access, documented retention, and adverse-event escalation protocols.

06 · Client implications

What the team should do next

Recommendations distinguish immediate, low-regret actions from questions that still require testing.

Act now · High confidence

Clarify the conversation value

Replace broad awareness messaging with a concise patient–HCP discussion aid that explains who may want to ask about the option and which questions to raise.

Test next · Medium confidence

Localize proof, not just language

Test benefit and trade-off framing separately by market before scaling. The pooled story conceals meaningful differences in what feels credible.

Monitor · Early signal

Track conversion quality

Add confidence-to-discuss and clarity measures to the next tracker wave; awareness growth is insufficient if it does not improve treatment conversations.

My recommended approach: Begin with a focused message test and patient–HCP discussion aid, then use the next tracking wave to evaluate whether clarity and conversation confidence improve before expanding the campaign.
